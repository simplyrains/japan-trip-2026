The route

Every driving day
under four hours.

Spend one night near Kirkjufell, finish the peninsula near Borgarnes, and sleep at Geysir after the Golden Circle. Two nights at Hali spread the lagoons and Vestrahorn across separate days. Keep both Vík stays and the final Reykjavík night. All 23 photo locations remain in the plan.

Built around your flight times.

Collect the car around 10:00 on Aug 7. Return it around 13:00 on Aug 15 and be inside KEF by 14:00 for the 17:00 flight. All times are Iceland local time, UTC+0; Taipei is 8 hours ahead and Thailand 7 hours ahead.

Daily driving cap: less than four hours.
