The route

Two nights in the west.
Then follow the south coast.

Start near Kirkjufell, cross the Golden Circle and continue to Vestrahorn before returning through the glacier lagoons. The final night is in Reykjavík, with an easy city morning and a firm airport buffer.

Built around your flight times.

Collect the car around 10:00 on Aug 7. Return it around 13:00 on Aug 15 and be inside KEF by 14:00 for the 17:00 flight. All times are Iceland local time, UTC+0; Taipei is 8 hours ahead and Thailand 7 hours ahead.
